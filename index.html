What I Do

Research

It started with simple questions and curiosity. Through different research projects, I learned how to turn ideas into structured studies—from collecting data and analyzing findings to writing and publishing the results.

That journey eventually led me to publish articles in SINTA 3 and SINTA 2 journals, an achievement that became one of the highlights of the academic journey.

Exploring questions, collecting data, and turning curiosity into research.

Education

My interest in education became more meaningful when I had the opportunity to step into a real school environment through Kampus Mengajar. I worked alongside teachers and students, supported learning activities, and took part in literacy, numeracy, and educational technology initiatives.

The experience taught me that education is not only about delivering knowledge—it is also about understanding people, adapting to different situations, and learning from each interaction.

User & Experience

Understanding people before designing for them.

My role in UI/UX is more focused on understanding people than creating pixels. I enjoy exploring user needs, gathering insights, and turning those findings into ideas that can help shape better experiences. Through UI/UX projects and competitions, I discovered that good design starts with understanding the people behind it.

Technology

Exploring how technology can turn ideas into something useful. Technology has always been something I enjoy exploring, even when I'm not the one writing every line of code. I often use AI tools to help me turn ideas into working prototypes and understand how different technologies can be applied.

For me, technology is less about being the best coder and more about knowing how to use the right tools to bring an idea to life.

Organization

University also taught me how much can happen behind the scenes. I joined various committees, taking roles in Publication & Documentation, Events, and later as a Steering Committee. I also became part of the GDGoC UNM Core Team in Finance.

From preparing events to managing responsibilities and working with different people, these experiences taught me how to collaborate, stay organized, and keep things moving even when things don't always go according to plan.

Working with people, managing moving parts, and making things happen.

Experience

Kampus Mengajar | SD Sudirman IV

During Kampus Mengajar Angkatan 8, I had the opportunity to work directly in a school environment, supporting teachers and students through various learning activities. I assisted teachers in preparing and carrying out classroom activities, supported students during lessons, and contributed to literacy and numeracy programs. I was also involved in school administration and documentation, helping with documents and other tasks needed to support daily school activities.

Working alongside teachers and students taught me how to communicate with different people, adapt to different situations, and make practical decisions when things did not always go according to plan. I also gained experience in utilizing educational technology to support learning activities. More than just teaching, the experience taught me that education is about understanding people, responding to their needs, and continuously learning from real-world interactions.

Praktik Industri | Dinas Kominfo dan Persandian Kab. Bulukumba

During my Industrial Practice at the Department of Communication, Informatics, and Encryption of Bulukumba Regency, I contributed to the digitalization of an administrative process through a website designed for non-ASN staff. My role included designing the interface in Figma, organizing information based on the required workflow, and implementing the website with the assistance of AI-based coding tools.

Through this experience, I learned how to translate administrative needs into a practical digital solution while considering the people who would use it. I also developed my understanding of user needs, interface design, problem-solving, and working in a professional environment.

Magang Mandiri | PT Digital Desa Indonesia (Digides)

During my independent internship at PT Digital Desa Indonesia (Digides), I worked as a Back Office Intern, supporting various administrative and operational activities related to the Digides digital village platform. My responsibilities included completing and updating village profiles and information, ensuring that the data displayed on the platform was organized and up to date. I also uploaded articles written by other interns, created tutorial videos to help explain the use of digital services, and assisted with other day-to-day back-office tasks.

This experience gave me a closer look at how digital platforms are managed behind the scenes and how accurate information supports the delivery of digital services. I learned to work carefully with information, maintain consistency when handling repetitive tasks, and manage responsibilities independently. It also strengthened my attention to detail, administrative skills, communication, and ability to work with digital platforms in a professional environment.

Selected Works

MUARA | Web Development

MUARA (Muna Barat Satisfaction Survey System)

MUARA (Muna Barat Satisfaction Survey System) is a web-based information system designed to facilitate public satisfaction surveys regarding population administration and civil registration services in West Muna Regency. The system was developed to provide a more structured and accessible way for users to submit their feedback and for service providers to collect and manage satisfaction data digitally.

Through this project, I was involved in the development of the system, from translating the required workflow into a functional web application to implementing its main features. MUARA was not only developed as a digital solution but also became the basis for an academic journal article, allowing the project to be documented and discussed from a research perspective.

Role: Web Development
Output: Web-based Information System & Academic Journal Article

BLOODSTREAM | UI/UX Design

BLOODSTREAM

Bloodstream is a UI/UX design concept for a blood donation application designed to make the process of finding blood donors and donation locations easier and more efficient. The application aims to help users quickly find nearby blood donation locations, access relevant information, and connect with available donors when blood is needed.

In this project, I focused on the user experience and research side of the design process, exploring user needs and considering how information could be presented in a simple and intuitive way. The design focuses on creating a clear user flow so that users can find the information they need without unnecessary steps, especially in situations where access to blood donors needs to be quick.

Role: UI/UX Research & Design
Focus: User needs, user flow, information structure, and interface design

PACE | User Research

PACE (Personal AI Care Engine)

PACE (Personal AI Care Engine) is a digital health application concept designed to help people better understand their health through an AI-powered personal health companion. The concept brings together early symptom assessment, health education, digital medical records, and access to healthcare professionals in one integrated platform.

In this project, I contributed as a User Researcher, focusing on understanding user problems, identifying healthcare-related needs, and exploring how the proposed solution could address common challenges such as misleading health information and incorrect self-diagnosis. The research helped shape the application's features and user-oriented approach, ensuring that the proposed solution was based on real user needs rather than technology alone.

The project was developed as a UI/UX competition proposal and successfully achieved 2nd Place in the Gorontalo Creativity Competition IT 2025.

My Focus: User Research, User Needs, Problem Identification, Solution Concept
Output: UI/UX Proposal & Digital Health Application Concept

Muslim Tracker | UI/UX Design

Muslim Tracker

Muslim Tracker is a mobile application concept designed to help users track their daily worship activities in a simple, private, and personal way. The application allows users to record and monitor their worship habits without the pressure of sharing their progress with others, making it a more personal space for self-reflection and consistency.

The project focuses on creating a simple and comfortable experience for users who want to build better worship habits. The interface and user flow were designed around the idea of making activity tracking easy to understand and effortless to use, while keeping personal worship records private.

Role: UI/UX Design

silapNON | Web Development

silapNON (Sistem Laporan Non-ASN)

silapNON is a web-based reporting system designed to help Non-ASN staff at the Department of Communication, Informatics, and Encryption of Bulukumba Regency submit their activity reports digitally. Research | First Author & Researcher

Journal Publications — SINTA 2 & SINTA 3

My research explores the application of technology in different contexts, from understanding university students’ relationship with artificial intelligence (AI) to applying image processing and artificial neural networks in agriculture. One study examined AI over-reliance and inert thinking among university students, focusing on the roles of performance expectancy and social influence. The study was published in a SINTA 2 journal.

Another project focused on classifying scallion freshness using digital image processing and artificial neural networks, published in a SINTA 3 journal. As the lead author of both studies, I contributed throughout the research process, from developing research ideas and methodologies to data processing, analysis, and academic writing.

Role: Lead Author & Researcher
Output: 2 Journal Publications — SINTA 2 & SINTA 3
